THE PROBLEM

DeFi yield shouldn't require constant monitoring.

Markets move constantly. ArbiAgent turns that continuous monitoring into a structured workflow — from market signals to authorized execution.

ArbiAgent connects market intelligence with controlled on-chain execution.
HOW IT WORKS

From AI intelligence to on-chain execution.

ArbiAgent separates the intelligence layer from the execution layer. The AI analyzes the market and signs a strategy signal, while the Vault validates and executes it on-chain.

PROTOCOL-READY INFRASTRUCTURE

Built beyond a single DeFi protocol.

Aave V3 is the first execution venue. The infrastructure is designed to evolve toward additional DeFi integrations without changing the core intelligence and authorization model.
